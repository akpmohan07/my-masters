# MSc Computing (Secure Software Engineering) at DCU: Study Materials

My coursework, notes, assignments and exam preparation from the MSc in Computing at
Dublin City University (2025–26). I'm sharing it so future students have somewhere to start.

> [!WARNING]
> **For educational use only. Be aware of plagiarism.**
>
> - Everything here is for **learning and reference**. Read it to understand a topic, then
>   write your own work.
> - **Do not submit any of this material, in whole or in part, as your own.** DCU checks
>   submissions with plagiarism-detection tools, which compare against public sources like
>   this repository. Copying is an academic integrity violation under DCU's policies and
>   can lead to a fail or worse.
> - If something here helps you, **cite it** like any other source.
> - Assignment briefs, marking schemes and module content change from year to year.
>   Something that was correct for 2025–26 may be wrong for your year.
> - No warranty: there will be mistakes. Check against your lecture material.

## What's deliberately *not* here

- **Lecture slides, lecture recordings and blank lab handouts.** They belong to DCU and
  the lecturers and are only available to enrolled students (on Loop).
- **Published papers.** Get them through the DCU library or the publisher.
